# Lab 1: Requirements and Use Case Modelling

Requirements and UML modelling for **Problem 19**, the Personalized Meal & Diet Subscription Manager (the same problem carried through Labs 1–3).

## What I did

- **Requirements table:** 5 functional and 2 non-functional requirements, each with a type, priority, measurable acceptance criteria and a rationale.
  - Functional: weekly meal plan generation within ±5% of the calorie goal, dietitian review and approval, daily health metric logging, delivery pause/skip/reschedule (more than 12 hours before dispatch), and delivery status tracking with notifications.
  - Non-functional: 2.5 s plan generation at 500 concurrent requests, and AES-256 at rest, TLS 1.3 in transit and RBAC for health data.
- **UML use case diagram** for the system.
- **Detailed use case UC-04, Review & Approve Meal Plan:** actors, preconditions, postconditions, a main success scenario and an alternate flow for overriding a meal item.
